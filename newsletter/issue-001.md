## Practice

Before you paste an error back to the AI, write one sentence: what you think is broken, and why.

Then paste. After the model answers, check your sentence. Keep it if it still holds; rewrite it if the answer changed your mind.

## Tool or mode tip

When you are learning something new, ask the model to question, hint, and check — not to finish the change. In an [Anthropic study of developers learning a new library](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills), people who looped errors back without a hypothesis scored lowest on the later quiz. That split was post hoc and the groups were small (n = 4), so treat it as a habit to try, not a proven recipe.

## Stuck of the week

Assisted practice can look like progress and still leave you worse on the unaided check. In a [math study with high-school students](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122), unrestricted AI help raised practice scores, then the same students did worse on a closed-book exam later that session. A tutor with safeguards mostly avoided the drop. That study is math, not coding; applying it here is our extrapolation.

A useful self-check: could you explain the last diff you approved?
