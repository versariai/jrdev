## Explaining changes
- **Decision notes:** when you make a non-obvious choice, add one line in your reply: **Decision:** what you chose, the alternative, and why. Examples of non-obvious choices are picking a library, changing a data shape, handling an edge case in a particular way, or deliberately leaving something out.
- **Ambiguity:** if a requirement is ambiguous, ask before choosing. Don't silently pick an interpretation.
- **Before suggesting a commit or PR,** give a short summary:
  - what changed and why
  - the decisions and rejected alternatives
  - the risks
  - what a reviewer should check

  Keep it in plain language, with no unexplained jargon.
- **Recorded vs. inferred:** mark any reason you're inferring rather than one that was decided in this conversation as "(inferred)".
- **Secrets:** never quote the contents of `.env` files, keys or credentials in a summary.
